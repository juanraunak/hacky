# The game — vision and World 1

## What this is

A game where you don't know you're learning. You're not answering a quiz. You don't even
feel like you're being taught. You're just in a world with your friends, doing things, and
by the end you understand something real — gravity, chemical bonding, whatever the world
is about. The knowledge is in the world, not in a pop-up.

Three worlds. One engine, one art style, one party of friends across all of them.

## Style (fixed, all three worlds)

- 3D, cel-shaded. Boruto (the Crunchyroll anime look — hard light bands, ink outlines)
  crossed with Mario's 3D style (bright, chunky, saturated, readable).
- Every character, including historical ones, is built in this same 3D style. Newton is a
  "3D bop" in our look, not a portrait.
- Full spec: `ART-STYLE.md`.

## Perspective

First person preferred. It needs mouse-look, and we don't know yet how long that takes.
Third person is the fallback. Decide after the first build, not before.

## Multiplayer

You drop in with your friends. The party is already there when the story starts. Everyone
sees the same tree, the same Newton, the same apple. Shared world, one link.

## Storytelling — text only, no voice

Nobody speaks out loud. Thoughts and dialogue appear as designed text — comic-style bubbles
in the art style. Characters *think* on screen, and the thinking is the story.

Example beat:

1. The apple falls on Newton.
2. Text: **"Ow."**
3. A pause. Then Newton starts thinking, in text, about why the apple came down — and keeps
   going. Why down. Why not sideways. Does the Moon fall too.

The player is not asked anything. They watch a mind work, and the idea lands the way it
landed on him.

## World 1 — Newton's tree

An open area. A big tree. Newton sitting against it. The party wanders in.

- The apple falls. The text beats above play out.
- The area around the tree is explorable. The far side is blocked off — you can see it,
  you can't get there. World 1 is bounded on purpose.
- Things to collect around the tree: TBD.

That's World 1. Nothing else goes in until this plays.
